Testing samplers

What the code in experiments/testing-samplers/ does, and what it found. 8-spin Ising rings (256 states, all computed exactly), sampled with THRML.

How the pieces fit together

Exact ground truth comes from enumerating all states; the real sampler is THRML; the harness compares the two.

Check 1 · Three distributions over all 256 states

States sorted by their exact probability. THRML's empirical frequencies (4,000 independent chains) follow the exact curve; the edge-by-edge lowering puts its mass in the wrong places.

Check 2 · p-values on a correct sampler

500 runs of THRML, each testing the mean energy against its exact value. For a correct sampler, p-values should be uniform (dashed line). The naive test piles up near 0, i.e. false alarms; batch means stays roughly flat.

Why: consecutive energy samples are correlated. The naive test treats 2,000 correlated samples as 2,000 independent ones.

Check 3 · Frozen chains

Mean spin of 8 chains on a cold ferromagnet (exact answer: 0), four started all up, four all down. R-hat on energy cannot see the problem; R-hat on magnetisation can.

Certificates · bounds vs. measured error

Coupling-capped sampler at β = 0.5. Every certificate stays above the true error; cheap local ones are loose, costlier exact ones tighter. A bound of 1 says nothing.
